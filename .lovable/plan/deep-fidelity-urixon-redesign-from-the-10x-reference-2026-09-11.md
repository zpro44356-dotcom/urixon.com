# Deep-fidelity Urixon redesign from the 10X reference

## Goal
Rework the current Urixon page so it captures the reference site's component composition, scale, pacing, animation choreography, and premium digital-studio feel much more closely—while keeping Urixon's monochrome black identity and Urixon content.

## What will change

### 1. Rebuild the opening composition
- Match the reference's compact floating navigation proportions, spacing, active indicator, and CTA treatment.
- Recreate the large orbital linework, edge crosshair details, centered badge, tightly controlled two-line headline, and staged supporting copy/buttons.
- Replace the current equal grid with an overlapping presentation cluster: large editorial photo, client-count tile with floating label, product/service tile, analytics chart tile, and featured automation/technology tile.
- Add the reference-style illuminated floor at the base of the cluster, translated into white and graphite instead of blue.

### 2. Capture the reference's section rhythm
- Use deliberate transitions between immersive dark areas and lifted monochrome surfaces rather than a sequence of similar bordered sections.
- Match its wide margins, oversized type moments, whitespace, cropped media, offset objects, and asymmetric balance.
- Preserve Urixon's services, projects, founder story, and calls to action while restructuring each block to follow the reference's visual hierarchy.

### 3. Rebuild components for closer fidelity
- About: oversized scroll-reactive statement, pale/dark word progression, floating image fragments, and layered depth.
- Approach: sticky left narrative with long scrolling numbered stages and stronger progress cues.
- Work: larger editorial case-study compositions with alternating dimensions, overlaid metadata, image reveal masks, and directional hover motion.
- Services: reference-style solution tiles with distinct visual mini-scenes rather than repeated accordion cards.
- Founder, testimonials, and insights: stronger asymmetric layouts, editorial typography, branded quote/navigation treatment, and more varied media geometry.
- Closing section and footer: large final statement, stronger conversion focus, and the reference's compact multi-column information density.

### 4. Rebuild the motion system
- Coordinate page-load animation as one sequence: navigation, badge, headline words, supporting text, buttons, then the visual cluster.
- Add masked text and image reveals, scroll-linked object drift, layered parallax, staggered cards, count-up values, and marquee movement.
- Add subtle cursor-responsive depth on desktop, refined arrow/button motion, image zoom, border/shine transitions, and active navigation feedback.
- Use varied durations and easing based on visual weight instead of applying one generic fade-up everywhere.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep mobile movement restrained and stable.

### 5. Refine the monochrome art direction
- Keep Urixon's near-black base, off-white type, graphite surfaces, and muted grey hierarchy.
- Introduce controlled tonal contrast so the page does not read as one flat dark layer.
- Convert the reference's blue energy into white light, silver bloom, and cool-grey depth—without adding blue.
- Tighten typography, corner radii, borders, shadows, grain, and image treatment into one consistent system.

## Technical details
- Refactor the existing site components rather than replacing the app structure.
- Expand the shared motion primitives for mask reveals, scroll-linked transforms, and reusable interaction choreography.
- Keep design values as semantic tokens in the global style system.
- Keep the experience as a single Urixon page and preserve its metadata.
- Verify the complete page at desktop and mobile sizes, including scroll behavior, menu interaction, service interactions, testimonial controls, text fit, overlaps, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Content boundary
Urixon's real content remains authoritative. Any generated imagery or sample testimonials/articles remain clearly replaceable placeholders; no unsupported business claims will be added.
